## Link Guard 0.14.0

This release gives Link Guard a new design that fits Thunderbird and shares its
layout with Link Guard for Outlook, and it closes a gap in how links that carry
another destination are shown.

New design: the notice is now a notification bar in Thunderbird's style. It names
what it found – suspicious links, links to blocked sites, or links waiting for
approval – and counts warnings, unapproved and blocked links. Expanded, it groups
the destination hosts into *Needs attention*, *Links that cannot be approved*,
*Not yet approved*, *Blocked* and a collapsed *Approved*. Each host has a card with
its registrable domain in bold, its status, warnings and hints, its actions, a **⋯**
menu and the list of its links with their full address and **Show in message**.
**Allow permanently** now approves the exact host directly; approving or blocking a
whole domain moved into the **⋯** menu. Light and dark mode follow Thunderbird.

Settings use the same look, with toggles and the Link Guard logo, which is now also
the add-on's icon in the Add-ons Manager.

Links that name a trusted site: a link such as `click.example.info/r?u=https://www.example.org/`
names a site you approved, but where it really leads is decided by `click.example.info`.
Such a link used to get no hint at all. It now shows an amber hint saying exactly that,
until the linking host itself is approved.

The permissions remain `messagesRead`, `scripting`, and `storage`. Thunderbird
151 or newer is required. The new interface texts in the 18 languages other than
English and German have not been reviewed by native speakers. See the [security policy](https://github.com/marco-schm/thunderbird-link-guard/blob/v0.14.0/SECURITY.md)
for limitations. The XPI and `SHA256SUMS` are built only after the YubiKey-signed
tag passes verification.
