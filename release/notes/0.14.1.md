## Link Guard 0.14.1

A fix for a question that went missing in 0.14.0.

**Allow permanently** and **Block** ask again which scope you mean: **Only
`www.example.org`** decides for exactly this host, **`example.org` and all subdomains**
for the whole registrable domain, and **Cancel** returns to the actions. In 0.14.0 both
buttons decided for the exact host at once, and the whole-domain choice was only in the
**⋯** menu, where it was easy to miss. The **⋯** menu keeps it as a shortcut.

The safety rules are unchanged: a host with a warning still needs its exact approval,
and shared-content hosts such as `docs.google.com` cannot be approved as a whole
domain. For these, **Allow permanently** approves the exact host at once, as before.
Blocking a whole domain is offered whenever the domain is known. The question is shown
in all 20 languages, with the wording of 0.13.

The permissions remain `messagesRead`, `scripting`, and `storage`. Thunderbird 151 or
newer is required. See the [security policy](https://github.com/marco-schm/thunderbird-link-guard/blob/v0.14.1/SECURITY.md)
for limitations. The XPI and `SHA256SUMS` are built only after the YubiKey-signed tag
passes verification.
